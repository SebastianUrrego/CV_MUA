Women’s Tailoring – ASFÓDELO

Inspiration – Fourth Semester

Asfódelo It is a capsule collection for Fall/Winter 2021, inspired by classical literature: “The Picture of Dorian Gray”. Through women’s tailoring, it represents duality, vanity, status, pride, and divinity in each outfit of the collection.

Aimed at a female audience aged 25 to 45, it stands out for its elegance and classic beauty. Materials used include drill, Italian suiting fabric, lightweight georgette, and textile paint.

MEN'S TAILORING

RACING Fifth semester

Racing It is a Fall/Winter 2022 capsule collection inspired by industrial technology applied to tires and how they have evolved over time for the benefit of humanity.

Said capsule targets a male audience aged 18 to 40, who are avant-garde eclectics seeking to make an impact with their looks.

The materials selected for this collection are Italian wool and synthetic leather in a neutral color range with electric accents that give it a distinctive touch.

LINGERIE, SWIMWEAR & SPORTSWEAR

ASTRALE Sixth semester

Astrale collection It is a Spring/Summer 2023 capsule collection inspired by the mountainous biome: the Gate of Orion; a rock formation located in San José del Guaviare, Colombia; a place where indigenous communities in the area used it as a meeting point for their rituals due to its high energy concentration related to the Orion constellation.

This collection is aimed at an avant-garde user profile for women between 17 and 40 years old, eclectic, unique, and confident.

HAUTE COUTURE
SKIN OF STORIES

"Skin of Stories" is a Spring/Summer 2024 collection inspired by African scarification. This cultural practice is characterized by having multiple meanings depending on the pattern of the scar and the area where it is performed. It represents the legacy of their ancestors and their life mission that identifies them as individuals who fulfill a role in their community.

This collection is aimed at an avant-garde, ethnic, and eclectic user profile as it merges the modernity of its materials, such as leather, with ancestral practices and fabrics that represent their many cultures full of tradition.

Zephir Store Collections

1. Tulia

Tulia, the first woman of the Zephir generation, was the thread that started it all. Together with her husband, she dreamed of a world woven with delicacy, with pieces that would embrace and endure over time. Passionate about accessories and detail, she found in each stitch a way to express her love for the beautiful and the well-made. Today, her essence lives on in every fiber of Zephir. This collection is a tribute to her and to all the women who, with their strength and sensitivity, have left their mark on our lives: mothers, grandmothers, sisters, and friends who, like Tulia, have woven our history with their hands and hearts.

Crafted from light and natural textiles, Tulia combines neutral tones with colorful accents to adapt to multiple occasions and urban lifestyles.

Tulia Collection

2. Alborada

At Zephir, we dedicate this collection to mothers, who have been our first home and constant guide.

The proposal seeks not only to dress but to envelop with warmth through comforting colors, cozy textures, and designs intended to last over time. This May, we celebrate 80 years of history by honoring the love that gives origin to our stories, the love that only a mother can transmit, in all its expressions.
